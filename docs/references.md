# Manufacturer and software references

Use the exact version/model of your device's manual. Links may change over time.

1. [LiftMaster LA400DC/LA400DCS Installation Manual](https://gatedepot.com/amfile/file/download/file/45813/product/19889/) — compatibility, expansion-board terminals, EXIT input, UL325 safety, battery disconnect, and service requirements.
2. [LiftMaster CSW24U Installation Manual, expansion board AUX relay table](https://www.movingupgaragedoors.com/amfile/file/download/file/1796/) — the same published OFF/OFF/ON Open Limit and OFF/ON/OFF Not-Closed combinations; **different operator**: cross-check with your own board/installation and verify contacts electrically.
3. [KinCony official KC868-A4 ESPHome package](https://github.com/hzkincony/kc868-a4/blob/main/kc868-a4.yaml) — GPIO2/15/5/4 relays and GPIO36/39/27/14 digital inputs.
4. [KinCony KC868-A4 firmware project](https://hzkincony.github.io/kc868-a4/) — vendor flashing information.
5. [ESPHome GPIO switch, momentary output and software interlock warnings](https://esphome.io/components/switch/gpio.html).
6. [ESPHome switch restore behavior](https://esphome.io/components/switch/).
7. [Home Assistant scripts](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/scripts/) and [button.press](https://www.home-assistant.io/actions/button.press/).
8. [Custom button-card](https://github.com/custom-cards/button-card) and [dynamic actions/confirmations](https://custom-cards.github.io/button-card/stable/config/actions/).
